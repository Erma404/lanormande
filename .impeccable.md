## Design Context

### Users
Families, groups of friends and guests planning an intimate celebration in Normandy. They are looking to check genuine availability and reserve a characterful home for up to eight people without being sent to a booking marketplace.

### Brand Personality
Rooted, generous, quietly refined. The experience should feel like being welcomed into a loved family home, never like browsing a generic hotel catalogue.

### Aesthetic Direction
An editorial, light-first Normandy home with warm linen surfaces, deep timber green, clay accents, and a single hearth-dark section for the floor plan. The Stayora reference informs the page rhythm and floating booking utility, while the visual language is anchored in half-timbering, gardens, old beams and a fireplace rather than coastal-villa luxury.

### Design Principles
- Let imagery and generous spacing make the house feel inhabited and calm.
- Make direct booking concrete, reassuring, and present from the first screen.
- Use asymmetrical editorial compositions rather than a uniform card grid.
- Keep every interaction legible and useful on a phone as well as on a wide screen.
- Build photo placeholders as simple replaceable image sources until the final property photography is available.
